    57. Question bank: 150 questions¶

    Depth: L2 · Reading time: ~11 min · Last verified: 2026-11-01 Prerequisites: Talking with product managers · Talking with engineering leaders and the CTO office

    TL;DR¶

    • 150 questions in 14 topics, each tagged by audience, distilled from every chapter's playbook and the staged ASK lines.
    • Bring five to seven to a meeting: one audience, one goal (Figure 57.4).
    • Strong answers carry a number, its conditions and its baseline. Forty-one "Listen for" notes show what to expect.
    • Differentiating questions sit on DirectFlash versus FDP, EXA versus shared media, and NVIDIA's context tier.
    • These are for colleagues. Many ask for internal telemetry; never use them to fish for competitors' non-public data.

    Why this matters for Everpure strategy¶

    A new strategy hire is judged by the questions they ask. "How fast is rebuild?" marks a tourist; "time to full redundancy at 80% fill, under load?" earns an engineer's respect and a number for the model. Most questions also transfer to diligence on a target (Chapter 50).

    The bank¶

    1. Media and NAND¶

    1. [PM] At today's flash premium, which workloads still convert from disk? ↳ Listen for: binding constraint per segment, break-even premium (Ch 52).
    2. [PM] How much NAND inflation is passed through versus absorbed?
    3. [CTO] Does owning raw NAND management let us adopt PLC first?
    4. [Eng] How do you manage read disturb on read-mostly AI data?
    5. [Eng] What does PLC cost the write path and endurance budget?
    6. [Eng] How long does qualifying a new NAND die take?
    7. [CTO] Which components are single-sourced, and what did Optane teach?
    8. [PM] How many quarters does the strategic NAND buy cover? ↳ Listen for: prepaid-versus-inventory split, contract-price assumption (Ch 48).
    9. [SE] Where disk was unavailable, did QLC win on availability?
    10. [Eng] What does a layer jump do to DFM cost per TB?

    2. SSD and DirectFlash¶

    1. [Eng] What field write amplification does Purity see on QLC? ↳ Listen for: measured ranges by workload, converted to DWPD headroom (Ch 3).
    2. [CTO] If FDP goes mainstream, which DirectFlash advantages survive? ↳ Listen for: concedes the WAF gap; quantifies DRAM avoided and W/TB (Ch 26).
    3. [Eng] Without module DRAM, where does the map live, per PB?
    4. [PM] When 512 TB standard SSDs ship, what keeps DFMs ahead?
    5. [Eng] How are LDPC read retries kept off the latency tail?
    6. [Eng] What is the blast radius of a DFM firmware bug?
    7. [PM] Why does FlashArray//ST use commercial SSDs?
    8. [SE] How do we explain usable-per-raw-TB against SSD-based arrays? ↳ Listen for: over-provisioning, reserve, parity and reduction separated (Ch 3).
    9. [Eng] How much raw NAND per effective TB do we save?
    10. [CTO] Could a hyperscaler licensee internalise the global FTL? ↳ Listen for: what keeps it dependent, and the AWS Nitro precedent (Ch 13).
    11. [PM] What limits 600 TB modules: dies, packaging, power or rebuild?

    3. Protocols and networking¶

    1. [PM] What is the host-attach mix, and how fast is NVMe/TCP growing?
    2. [Eng] What is the NVMe/TCP versus RoCE gap at QD1 and saturation?
    3. [Eng] What are NVMe-oF connection limits with thousands of Kubernetes hosts?
    4. [PM] Why pNFS for EXA, and what does it cost us? ↳ Listen for: no kernel module; candour on shared-file writes (Ch 8).
    5. [SE] Which distros and kernels does the EXA client support?
    6. [Eng] A data node fails mid-write: what happens to in-flight I/O?
    7. [PM] Is S3 over RDMA generally available, and cuObject-compatible?
    8. [Eng] In S3 over RDMA, how are tokens scoped without TLS?
    9. [SE] What happens to RoCE on a lossy fabric without PFC?
    10. [Eng] How do our storage ports absorb synchronized checkpoint incast?
    11. [PM] How often does an InfiniBand compute fabric cost us deals?
    12. [CTO] How would our RDMA protocols map onto Ultra Ethernet?

    4. Architecture¶

    1. [Eng] Walk a 4 KB write: persistence point and per-layer p99? ↳ Listen for: NVRAM persistence point (mirrored or shared), per-layer microseconds (Ch 13).
    2. [PM] What does a customer lose during an Evergreen controller swap?
    3. [Eng] Under Purity Turbo, how does the secondary's read cache stay coherent?
    4. [PM] When a block customer outgrows one //XL, what do we sell?
    5. [Eng] Is the next FlashArray ceiling CPU, memory, PCIe or NVRAM?
    6. [PM] Where does EXA beat shared-media designs, and where not? ↳ Listen for: throughput, GPU-count and namespace thresholds; credit to VAST (Ch 16).
    7. [Eng] How many fabric crossings precede an EXA write acknowledgement?
    8. [Eng] An EXA data node dies mid-checkpoint: what do clients see? ↳ Listen for: protection scheme, degraded-throughput percentage, rebuild time (Ch 27).
    9. [Eng] 16,384 ranks create files in one directory: what happens? ↳ Listen for: directory hashing, lock granularity, creates/s at p99 (Ch 21).
    10. [CTO] Is the metadata/data split permanent, or could shared media return?
    11. [Eng] Which services run consensus, and what stalls without quorum?
    12. [SE] A neocloud says "we'll run Ceph": what's our TCO counter? ↳ Listen for: staff, rebuild windows, free-space reserve, watts per usable TB (Ch 15).

    5. Data services and protection¶

    1. [PM] What are fleet median reduction ratios by workload? ↳ Listen for: distributions excluding thin provisioning; candour on AI data (Ch 5).
    2. [Eng] How big is the dedupe index per PB, and where?
    3. [Eng] When does inline reduction defer, and for how long?
    4. [Eng] What read amplification does similarity encoding add?
    5. [SE] How does the Right-Size Guarantee treat irreducible data?
    6. [Eng] A large DFM dies at 80% fill: time to full redundancy? ↳ Listen for: hours under load, host impact, declustered or not (Ch 6).
    7. [Eng] What if ActiveCluster's mediator and link fail together?
    8. [Eng] What stops stolen credentials shortening SafeMode retention? ↳ Listen for: out-of-band authentication, multiple approvers, audit trail (Ch 6).
    9. [PM] In ransomware recovery, how much of the RTO is storage?
    10. [CTO] What is in the denominator of our six nines?

    6. Everpure products¶

    1. [PM] Where does //ST win deals that //XL190 would lose?
    2. [PM] What share of EXA pipeline awaits DFM nodes or certification? ↳ Listen for: enterprise and neocloud gates, each with a date (Ch 27).
    3. [Eng] How are EXA data nodes protected today?
    4. [SE] Who beats FlashBlade//E, and on what metric?
    5. [PM] For VMs moving to KubeVirt, where does Portworx win?
    6. [Eng] What does Portworx lose on non-Everpure disks?
    7. [PM] Is Fusion adoption "enabled" or "presets in production"? ↳ Listen for: turned-on versus automating, tied to renewal (Ch 28).
    8. [Eng] How does Fusion pick an array for a workload?
    9. [SE] If Pure1 and Fusion are unreachable, what still works?
    10. [CTO] What may an agent do via MCP without human approval? ↳ Listen for: read-only defaults, scoped credentials, approval gates (Ch 22).
    11. [PM] What share of Sev-1 cases does Pure1 open first?
    12. [CTO] Which Purity code came from acquisitions, and how much was rewritten?

    7. Platform, Evergreen and consumption¶

    1. [PM] What is Evergreen//One gross margin by contract year? ↳ Listen for: early hardware drag, later payback, utilization assumption (Ch 23).
    2. [PM] How much deployed headroom sits idle, at whose cost?
    3. [CTO] What happens to //One margin when reduction underdelivers?
    4. [PM] How is //One capacity growth priced after NAND inflation? ↳ Listen for: exposure first, then repricing and density offsets (Ch 48).
    5. [PM] Why do customers pick Evergreen//Forever over //One?
    6. [Eng] What breaks non-disruptive upgrades at generation boundaries?
    7. [Eng] Is consumption metered on provisioned, written or effective capacity?
    8. [SE] Which //One SLAs fail at dark sites?
    9. [Eng] Is the watts-per-TiB guarantee measured or modelled?
    10. [CTO] Does subscription reweight the roadmap toward density and watts?

    8. AI training and checkpointing¶

    1. [PM] In lost training deals, how was checkpointing specified? ↳ Listen for: framework, checkpoint size, burst versus time-to-resume (Ch 31).
    2. [Eng] For a ~6 TB checkpoint, how long are GPUs blocked?
    3. [Eng] Cold 16K-rank restore: opens/s, GB/s, time to first step? ↳ Listen for: metadata and data separated; slowest-client tail (Ch 31).
    4. [Eng] Why was EXA's MLPerf v3.0 checkpoint read below write?1 ↳ Listen for: cache-clear rule, client DRAM, bottleneck named (Ch 37).
    5. [SE] Which tool turns model size and GPU count into EXA nodes?
    6. [SE] How do we size for async checkpointing to local NVMe? ↳ Listen for: drain rate, restore bandwidth, retention, not peak write (Ch 31).
    7. [CTO] As async checkpointing spreads, where does differentiation move?
    8. [Eng] What happens when billions of small files reshuffle each epoch?
    9. [PM] Why enter MLPerf checkpointing and KV cache, but not training?

    9. Inference and KV cache¶

    1. [PM] What baseline sits behind PureKVA's 20× TTFT claim?2 ↳ Listen for: model, context, concurrency; recompute or local NVMe (Ch 32).
    2. [Eng] What GB/s per GPU and p99 per block at 1,000 sessions?
    3. [Eng] Is our NIXL backend upstream, and which plugin path?
    4. [Eng] What happens to cached KV when weights change?
    5. [CTO] Versus NVIDIA CMX: supplier, tier behind it, or rival?3 ↳ Listen for: a position per deal type, value beyond flash (Ch 34).
    6. [PM] Price a KV tier per TB, per GB-hour or per token?
    7. [Eng] Does DeepReduce shrink fp8 KV tensors at all?
    8. [SE] How do we answer DDN's and WEKA's KV-cache multiples? ↳ Listen for: baseline, context and concurrency normalised first (Ch 44).
    9. [PM] Does PureKVA ride existing FlashBlade deals?
    10. [Eng] Where does a KV fetch's latency budget break first?

    10. RAG and data intelligence¶

    1. [PM] In RAG on FlashBlade, where does the vector index live?
    2. [Eng] How does a billion-vector DiskANN index perform on FlashBlade?
    3. [PM] Which engines run Iceberg on FlashBlade in production?
    4. [Eng] Can random Lance reads and Parquet scans share a bucket?
    5. [PM] What share of Data Intelligence covers non-Everpure data?4 ↳ Listen for: a number, named estates, separate pricing (Ch 39).
    6. [PM] Who buys Data Intelligence, and who carries quota?
    7. [CTO] Catalog of record, or neutral feeder to Unity Catalog? ↳ Listen for: an explicit choice; coexistence without rip-out (Ch 33).
    8. [Eng] How does the MCP server handle identity and revocation?
    9. [Eng] Is classification on the write path or off it?
    10. [CTO] Is agent memory a storage workload beyond KV cache?

    11. NVIDIA and ecosystem¶

    1. [PM] Beyond NVCS validation, what gates EXA's NCP and SuperPOD certification? ↳ Listen for: level, GPU generation, gating tests, waiting deals (Ch 34).
    2. [SE] What headroom over SuperPOD storage sizing, and what limits it?
    3. [Eng] What did certification skip that production hits?
    4. [CTO] Which Purity services fit on a BlueField-4 DPU? ↳ Listen for: data path versus metadata and dedupe; memory limits (Ch 53).
    5. [PM] How do we enter a Dell- or HPE-led AI factory?
    6. [PM] What share of AI pipeline is NVIDIA-led, OEM-led or direct?
    7. [CTO] How long does each GPU-generation recertification take?
    8. [SE] Which tenancy gaps block EXA in neocloud bake-offs? ↳ Listen for: keys, VRF, QoS floors, metering, each dated (Ch 35).
    9. [SE] How do we stop one tenant's checkpoint starving another?
    10. [CTO] How do we become a named NCP design, not just certified?

    Conversation playbook¶

    Questions for a PM (follow-ups to any answer)

    1. "What's the denominator?" Strong: population, period, exclusions.
    2. "Against what baseline?" Strong: recompute, disk, or a named rival.
    3. "Which deals did it decide?" Strong: counts, not anecdotes.

    Questions for an engineer

    1. "Under what conditions?" Strong: fill, load, failure state.
    2. "What saturates first?" Strong: one resource, one number.
    3. "What if it fails halfway?" Strong: recovery path and client view.

    Don't say this:

    • Reading questions verbatim from a list.
    • Accepting a number without its conditions.
    • Treating "we don't measure that" as evasion; it may be the finding.
