# CLAUDE.md — Everpure Tech Atlas

This repository is the **Everpure Tech Atlas**: a ≥100,000-word, visual-heavy knowledge base on storage and AI storage. It is written for a corporate development / strategy professional (CFA, MBA, legal background, semiconductor M&A experience) joining Strategy & Corporate Development at **Everpure** (formerly Pure Storage, renamed February 2026, NYSE: PSTG). The reader knows chips, NAND basics, datacenter economics and deal work. They do not know storage systems, protocols or storage software in depth.

**Goal:** let the reader hold credible, efficient conversations with Everpure product managers, engineering leaders and the Office of the CTO, by understanding *how things work, why the trade-offs exist, and what they mean strategically*.

## Depth labels
- **L1 – Vocabulary:** can define it and use it in a sentence.
- **L2 – Mechanism:** can whiteboard how it works, name the key trade-offs, and say why one design beats another for a workload. **← Default depth.**
- **L3 – Implementation:** code paths, on-disk formats, kernel internals. Only in a short "Going deeper" box.

Use semiconductor analogies as a bridge: memory hierarchies, HBM/DRAM, NAND physics, controllers, PCIe, SerDes, interconnects, chiplets, foundry/supply chain, GPU utilization economics.

## Execution mode: one uninterrupted run
- Build the whole atlas in one run, from empty repo to the Definition of Done. Do not stop for review or ask questions.
- Ambiguity → make the most reasonable call, log it in `meta/decisions.md`, continue.
- Unverifiable fact → `[VERIFY: …]` inline + log in `meta/open-questions.md`, continue.
- After any context compaction: re-read `CLAUDE.md` and `PROGRESS.md`, then continue with the next unfinished item.
- Stop only at Definition of Done or a hard blocker. Record any blocker in `PROGRESS.md` first.

## Standing rules
1. **Accuracy over volume.** Never invent numbers, product names, specs, dates, customers, funding rounds, valuations or quotes. Every quantitative or time-sensitive claim gets an inline footnote with source, publication date and retrieval date. If unverifiable, write `[VERIFY: …]` and log it.
2. **Research live.** Use web search/fetch for the current state of the world (products, specs, financials, competitors, funding, share, partnerships, roadmaps). Treat training data as stale after mid-2025.
3. **Source priority:** (a) company primary sources (SEC filings, earnings calls, investor decks, docs, datasheets, white papers, engineering blogs); (b) standards bodies (SNIA, NVM Express, IETF/NFS RFCs, CXL Consortium, MLCommons); (c) NVIDIA and partner documentation; (d) academic/industry papers (USENIX FAST/OSDI/SOSP, Hot Chips, FMS); (e) reputable trade press and analysts. For private companies (VAST, WEKA, DDN) label figures *company-stated*, *reported by press* or *estimated*. Avoid SEO content and forums.
4. **Copyright.** Own words only. Never paste vendor text or reproduce vendor diagrams; redraw concepts in original Mermaid/SVG. Quotes < 15 words, max one quote per source.
5. **Public information only.** No speculation about non-public Everpure plans; nothing confidential. Strategy sections are framed as *"options a strategy team might evaluate, based on public information."*
6. **Visual density:** ≥ 1 visual per ~700 words; every chapter ≥ 3 visuals (and ≥ 2 that are not tables). A visual = Mermaid diagram, SVG, generated chart (PNG/SVG + data CSV), or structured comparison table.
7. **Voice:** sharp senior analyst briefing a smart executive. Plain English, concrete examples, worked numbers, no marketing fluff, no vendor cheerleading (including for Everpure). Show trade-offs; steelman competitors.
8. **Every chapter follows the Chapter Template** (`meta/style-guide.md`). No exceptions.
9. **Consistency:** use terms from `docs/glossary.md`; add new terms (via `meta/glossary-staging/chNN.md`); cross-link chapters with relative Markdown links.
10. **Commit continuously.** One commit per chapter (`docs(ch42): VAST Data deep dive`); push after each batch. Update `PROGRESS.md` after every chapter.
11. **Substance, not padding.** Word budgets are met with mechanisms, examples, numbers, history and implications — never repetition.
12. **Never stop for review.**

## Repo conventions (see `meta/decisions.md` for why)
- Repo root = atlas root (the repo is `storage-_db_claude`; the atlas is "everpure-tech-atlas").
- Chapters: `docs/<NN-part>/<NN>-<slug>.md`. Canonical list in `meta/outline.md`.
- Images live in `docs/assets/{diagrams,charts}/` so relative paths work on both github.com and MkDocs.
- Canonical glossary: `docs/glossary.md`. Root `glossary.md` and `docs/index.md` are generated by `scripts/sync_root.py` — don't hand-edit them.
- Chart data: `data/*.csv` (must include `source` and `as_of` columns). One chart module per chart in `scripts/charts/*.py`; `scripts/make_charts.py` runs them all.
- Footnotes: Markdown footnotes `[^n]`, defined in the chapter's `## Sources` section.
