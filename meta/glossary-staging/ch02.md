- **Memory–storage wall** — The roughly 1,000× latency jump between DRAM-class memory (~100 ns) and NAND flash (~100 µs) that storage-system design works to hide. | 2
- **HBM (high-bandwidth memory)** — Stacked DRAM placed on the same package as a GPU or accelerator, the fastest and most expensive tier of the memory hierarchy. | 2
- **CXL memory** — DRAM attached over a Compute Express Link (PCIe-based) connection, adding capacity at roughly two to three times local DRAM latency. | 2
- **Charge-trap cell** — The 3D NAND cell design that stores electrons in a silicon-nitride layer around a vertical channel instead of in a floating gate. | 2
- **NAND string** — A column of NAND cells connected in series along one vertical channel between a bit line and a source line. | 2
- **P/E cycle** — One program/erase cycle of a NAND block; the unit in which flash wear and endurance are counted. | 2
- **Program/erase asymmetry** — NAND's property that data is programmed page by page but can only be erased a whole (much larger) block at a time, so it cannot be overwritten in place. | 2
- **DWPD (drive writes per day)** — An SSD endurance rating: how many times the drive's full capacity can be written each day over its warranty period. | 2
- **Data retention** — How long NAND holds data without power before charge leakage causes errors; it shrinks as cells wear and as temperature rises. | 2
- **Read disturb** — Gradual shifting of unread NAND cells caused by the pass voltage applied during reads of other cells in the same block, forcing periodic data relocation. | 2
- **SLC / MLC / TLC / QLC / PLC** — NAND storing 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 bits per cell (2, 4, 8, 16 or 32 voltage states), trading endurance and speed for cost per bit. | 2
- **SLC cache** — Part of a TLC or QLC SSD run in 1-bit mode to absorb incoming writes quickly before they are folded into denser cells. | 2
- **3D NAND deck** — One separately etched section of a tall 3D NAND layer stack; high-layer parts stack two or three decks. | 2
- **Wafer bonding (NAND)** — Building the NAND's CMOS control logic on a separate wafer and bonding it to the memory array wafer. | 2
- **Areal density** — Bits stored per unit area of a disk platter or chip, the main driver of cost per bit. | 2
- **SMR (shingled magnetic recording)** — HDD recording that overlaps tracks like roof shingles for more capacity, at the cost of having to rewrite whole bands or zones sequentially. | 2
- **HAMR (heat-assisted magnetic recording)** — HDD recording that briefly heats a tiny spot with a laser so that very stable, high-density magnetic media can be written. | 2
- **CMR / ePMR** — Conventional (non-overlapping) perpendicular magnetic recording, including energy-assisted variants, the mainstream HDD technology before HAMR. | 2
- **LTO (Linear Tape-Open)** — The dominant open tape-cartridge standard; LTO-10 holds 30 TB native per cartridge. | 2
