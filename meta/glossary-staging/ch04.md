- **IOPS** — Input/output operations completed per second, usually quoted for small (4 KiB) random reads. | 4
- **Throughput** — Data moved per second (e.g., GB/s), equal to IOPS multiplied by I/O size; usually quoted for large sequential transfers. | 4
- **Latency** — The time from issuing one storage request to its completion, measured in microseconds for flash and milliseconds for disk. | 4
- **Queue depth** — The number of I/O requests outstanding at a device or system at one time. | 4
- **Little's law** — The queueing identity L = λW: requests in flight equal arrival rate times time in system, so outstanding I/Os = IOPS × latency. | 4
- **Utilization** — The fraction of time a resource (die, CPU, port, disk arm) is busy; latency rises sharply as it approaches 100%. | 4
- **Tail latency** — The latency of the slowest small fraction of requests, expressed as a high percentile such as p99 or p99.9. | 4
- **p99 / p99.9** — The latency that 1% or 0.1% of requests exceed. | 4
- **Fan-out** — Splitting one request into many parallel sub-requests, so that the slowest sub-request determines overall latency. | 4
- **IOPS per TB** — A device's or system's random IOPS divided by its capacity; the metric that falls as hard drives grow. | 4
- **Raw capacity** — The sum of the drives' capacities in decimal terabytes, before any protection or overhead. | 4
- **Usable capacity** — Capacity left after parity, rebuild spares, metadata and system reserves are deducted from raw. | 4
- **Effective capacity** — Usable capacity multiplied by an assumed data-reduction ratio. | 4
- **Data-reduction ratio (DRR)** — Logical data stored divided by physical capacity consumed after deduplication and compression. | 4
- **Total efficiency** — A vendor ratio that adds thin-provisioning and snapshot savings on top of data reduction, and is therefore larger and softer than DRR. | 4
- **TiB (tebibyte)** — 2^40 bytes, about 9% smaller than a decimal terabyte (10^12 bytes). | 4
- **TB/W** — Storage capacity per watt of power drawn, a key efficiency metric for power-constrained data centres. | 4
- **TB/RU** — Storage capacity per rack unit (1.75 inches of rack height), a density metric. | 4
- **PUE (power usage effectiveness)** — Total facility power divided by IT equipment power; 1.0 would mean zero cooling and distribution overhead. | 4
- **Total cost of ownership (TCO)** — All costs of a system over a stated period: acquisition, power, space, support, refresh and operations. | 4
