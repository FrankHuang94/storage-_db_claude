- **Data-reduction ratio (DRR)** — Logical data written by hosts divided by the physical capacity it consumes after reduction, before RAID or erasure-coding overhead. | 5
- **Deduplication** — Storing identical chunks of data once and replacing repeats with pointers found by fingerprint lookup. | 5
- **Fingerprint** — A cryptographic hash of a data chunk used as its identity in a deduplication index. | 5
- **Fixed-block chunking** — Cutting data into chunks at fixed offsets such as every 4 KB or 8 KB, which is cheap but misses duplicates after insertions shift the data. | 5
- **Content-defined chunking (CDC)** — Cutting data where a rolling hash over the content matches a pattern, so chunk boundaries survive insertions. | 5
- **Rolling hash** — A hash recomputed cheaply as a window slides one byte at a time, used to find content-defined chunk boundaries (e.g., Rabin, Gear). | 5
- **Similarity reduction** — Storing near-identical blocks as compressed differences against a reference block found via a locality-sensitive hash. | 5
- **Pattern removal** — Detecting all-zero or repeating-pattern blocks and recording them as metadata instead of storing them. | 5
- **Inline reduction** — Data reduction performed before data is written to persistent media. | 5
- **Post-process reduction** — Data reduction performed in the background after data has landed on media. | 5
- **Thin provisioning** — Allocating physical capacity to a volume only when data is actually written, rather than at creation. | 5
- **Total efficiency** — A vendor ratio that adds thin-provisioning and snapshot savings to data reduction, and therefore overstates true data reduction. | 5
- **Right-Size Guarantee** — Everpure's (originally Pure Storage's, 2016) workload-based guarantee of effective capacity for FlashArray purchases. | 5
- **Data reduction guarantee** — A vendor contract promising a minimum reduction ratio or effective capacity, usually on reducible data only, with extra capacity as the remedy. | 5
- **Purity DeepReduce** — Everpure's similarity-based, cluster-wide, asynchronous data reduction for FlashBlade, introduced in 2026. | 5
- **Bloom filter** — A compact probabilistic structure that answers "definitely not present" cheaply, used to avoid deduplication index lookups for new data. | 5
- **Reducible data** — Data not already compressed or encrypted by the host, which is what most reduction guarantees cover. | 5
