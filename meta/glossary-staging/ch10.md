- **RDMA (remote direct memory access)** — A transport in which the network card moves data directly between registered memory regions on two machines, with no kernel copies and no remote CPU involvement. | 10
- **RNIC** — A network interface card that implements the RDMA transport in hardware. | 10
- **Queue pair** — The send/receive work-queue pair through which an application posts RDMA operations directly to the NIC. | 10
- **Kernel bypass** — Moving data between an application and the NIC without system calls, interrupts or kernel socket buffers on the data path. | 10
- **Zero-copy** — Transferring data between memory and the wire without the CPU copying it between buffers. | 10
- **InfiniBand** — A purpose-built, credit-based lossless network fabric for HPC and AI, today supplied effectively only by NVIDIA (Quantum). | 10
- **RoCEv2** — RDMA over Converged Ethernet version 2: the InfiniBand transport carried in routable UDP/IP packets on Ethernet. | 10
- **PFC (Priority Flow Control)** — An Ethernet mechanism that pauses a link for one traffic class to prevent drops, at the risk of spreading congestion. | 10
- **ECN (Explicit Congestion Notification)** — Switches mark packets when queues build so senders can slow down before drops occur. | 10
- **DCQCN** — The standard ECN-based congestion-control algorithm for RoCEv2, in which the sender NIC cuts its rate on congestion notifications. | 10
- **Incast** — Many senders converging on one receiver port at once, the typical overload pattern in storage networks. | 10
- **Front-end / back-end network** — In AI clusters, the north-south network for users, ingest and often storage, versus the east-west fabric for GPU-to-GPU traffic. | 10
- **DPU (data processing unit)** — A NIC with its own general-purpose CPU complex and accelerators that can run storage, security or networking software. | 10
- **SuperNIC** — NVIDIA's term for a high-bandwidth NIC tuned for AI east-west traffic, such as ConnectX-8/9. | 10
- **Spectrum-X** — NVIDIA's Ethernet platform pairing Spectrum switches with its NICs for adaptive routing and congestion control on AI fabrics. | 10
- **Ultra Ethernet (UEC / UET)** — An industry consortium and its 2025 specification for an Ethernet-based AI/HPC transport that tolerates lossy fabrics. | 10
- **BlueField-4 STX** — NVIDIA's 2026 modular storage reference architecture in which storage services run on BlueField-4 DPUs. | 10
- **CMX** — NVIDIA's rack-scale context-memory storage platform for KV cache, the first implementation of STX. | 10
- **SerDes** — Serializer/deserializer circuits whose per-lane rate (e.g., 112G, 224G) determines achievable port speeds. | 10
- **PAM4** — Four-level pulse-amplitude modulation, carrying two bits per symbol to double lane rates over NRZ. | 10
