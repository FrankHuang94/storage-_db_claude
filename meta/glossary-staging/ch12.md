- **PAM4** — Four-level pulse-amplitude modulation that carries two bits per symbol, used by PCIe 6.0 and later. | 12
- **FLIT** — The fixed 256-byte transfer unit of PCIe 6.0+ and CXL 3.x, carrying data plus FEC and CRC bytes. | 12
- **Retimer** — An active chip that recovers and re-transmits a high-speed serial signal to extend link reach. | 12
- **CXL (Compute Express Link)** — An open interconnect that runs cache-coherent memory and I/O protocols over the PCIe physical layer. | 12
- **CXL Type 3 device** — A CXL memory expander: a controller plus DRAM that the host reaches with ordinary loads and stores. | 12
- **CXL.mem** — The CXL protocol that lets a host read and write a device's memory at cache-line granularity. | 12
- **Host-managed device memory (HDM)** — Device-attached memory mapped into the host's physical address space, typically exposed as a CPU-less NUMA node. | 12
- **Back-invalidate** — A CXL 3.0 mechanism that lets a memory device recall cache lines from hosts, enabling coherent multi-host sharing. | 12
- **Memory pooling** — Assigning slices of switch-attached memory to different hosts on demand to reduce stranded DRAM. | 12
- **EDSFF** — The SNIA family of data-centre SSD form factors (E1.S, E1.L, E3.S, E3.L) designed for density, airflow and scalable power. | 12
- **E1.S** — A small EDSFF form factor suited to dense compute and GPU servers. | 12
- **E3.L** — A long EDSFF form factor with up to 70 W envelope, used for high-capacity QLC drives. | 12
- **Flexible Data Placement (FDP)** — An NVMe feature in which hosts tag writes with placement hints so data with similar lifetimes share erase blocks. | 12
- **Zoned Namespaces (ZNS)** — An NVMe command set that exposes zones which must be written sequentially and reset by the host. | 12
- **Write amplification factor (WAF)** — The ratio of physical NAND writes to host writes, driven mainly by garbage collection. | 12
- **Computational storage** — Placing processing in or beside a storage device to operate on data before it crosses the host interface. | 12
- **Storage-class memory (SCM)** — Persistent media faster than NAND but cheaper than DRAM, such as Intel Optane (3D XPoint). | 12
- **3D XPoint** — The phase-change-based persistent memory technology behind Intel Optane, co-developed with Micron. | 12
