- **Capex purchase** — Buying storage outright as a capital asset that the customer owns, depreciates and eventually replaces. | 23
- **Operating lease** — Financing in which a lessor owns the hardware and the customer pays periodic rent, recorded under ASC 842 as a right-of-use asset and lease liability. | 23
- **Storage-as-a-service (STaaS)** — A model in which the vendor owns and operates storage at the customer's site and bills for consumption against service-level agreements. | 23
- **Evergreen//Forever** — Everpure's subscription for customer-owned arrays that includes in-place controller refreshes at each three-year renewal (formerly Evergreen Gold). | 23
- **Evergreen//Flex** — Everpure's fleet-level program that bills capacity per use across a customer's arrays. | 23
- **Evergreen//One** — Everpure's storage-as-a-service offering (formerly Pure-as-a-Service), with SLAs on performance, availability, capacity and energy. | 23
- **Ever Modern** — The Evergreen//Forever entitlement to new-generation controllers with each three-year subscription renewal (six years for //E products). | 23
- **Non-disruptive upgrade (NDU)** — Replacing controllers, software or media while the system keeps serving I/O, with no data migration. | 23
- **Ratable revenue recognition** — Recognizing revenue evenly over the service period rather than upfront at delivery. | 23
- **ASC 606** — The US GAAP revenue-recognition standard that times revenue to the transfer of control of goods or the delivery of services. | 23
- **ASC 842** — The US GAAP lease standard that puts most leases on the lessee's balance sheet and defines when a contract contains a lease. | 23
- **Substitution right** — A supplier's contractual right to swap the underlying asset; if substantive, it can mean a contract contains no lease. | 23
- **TCV (total contract value)** — The full value of a contract signed in a period, regardless of when revenue is recognized. | 23
- **RPO (remaining performance obligations)** — Contracted revenue not yet recognized, disclosed under ASC 606. | 23
- **Subscription ARR** — The annualized run rate of recurring subscription revenue at period end. | 23
- **Net dollar retention (NDR)** — Recurring revenue from an existing customer cohort this year as a percentage of the same cohort's revenue a year earlier. | 23
