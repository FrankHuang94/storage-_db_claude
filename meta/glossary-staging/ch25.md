- **All Software Included** — Pure Storage's original licensing model in which every Purity software feature came with the array at no extra charge. | 25
- **Forever Flash** — Pure Storage's 2014 maintenance programme that bundled controller refreshes with maintenance renewals, the predecessor of Evergreen. | 25
- **Evergreen** — Everpure's family of subscriptions (//Forever, //Flex, //One) that replaces periodic forklift array replacements with non-disruptive upgrades and consumption options. | 25
- **Evergreen//Forever** — The Evergreen tier for customer-owned arrays with included non-disruptive controller and capacity upgrades (formerly Evergreen Gold). | 25
- **Evergreen//Flex** — The Evergreen tier in which customers own the hardware but pay for capacity per use across a fleet. | 25
- **Evergreen//One** — Everpure's storage-as-a-service subscription, billed on consumed capacity with service-level commitments. | 25
- **Forklift upgrade** — Replacing an entire storage system and migrating its data, the incumbent refresh model that Evergreen was designed to remove. | 25
- **Design win (hyperscaler)** — A hyperscaler's commitment to build its storage on a supplier's technology, in Everpure's case licensed DirectFlash and Purity rather than finished arrays. | 25
- **Technology licensing model** — A commercial structure in which the customer sources its own hardware and NAND and pays the supplier for software, IP and support. | 25
- **Enterprise Data Cloud (EDC)** — Everpure's 2025 architecture that uses Pure Fusion to manage a whole on-premises and cloud fleet as one policy-driven pool. | 25
- **Everpure Data Intelligence** — The data discovery, classification and semantic-context product built from the 1touch acquisition, GA in June 2026. | 25
- **Everpure Data Stream** — Everpure's GPU-accelerated ingest-to-inference data pipeline built on NVIDIA's AI Data Platform reference design. | 25
- **ObjectEngine** — Pure Storage's short-lived backup appliance built on StorReduce deduplication technology, launched in 2019 and de-emphasised by mid-2020. | 25
- **ASC 606** — The US revenue-recognition standard adopted by Pure Storage from fiscal 2019, which restated earlier figures and limits comparability across the change. | 25
- **Subscription ARR** — Everpure's annualised recurring revenue from subscription services and Evergreen//One, a key metric for the subscription transition. | 25
- **Tuck-in acquisition** — A small acquisition that buys a capability or team to fold into an existing product rather than buying a large revenue base. | 25
