- **AI data pipeline** — The end-to-end flow of data through ingest, curation, training, checkpointing, fine-tuning, inference, RAG and agents, each with its own storage I/O pattern. | 30
- **Data lake** — A low-cost, usually object-based store that holds raw and curated data for analytics and AI. | 30
- **Hot tier** — The high-performance file or flash storage tier that feeds GPUs directly during training and inference. | 30
- **Data curation** — Filtering, deduplicating, cleaning and tokenizing raw data into a training-ready dataset. | 30
- **Data loader** — The training-framework component that reads, decodes and prefetches batches of samples from storage for the GPUs. | 30
- **Shard** — A large file or object that packs many training samples together so storage serves big sequential reads instead of many small ones. | 30
- **Token** — The unit of text (roughly a word fragment) that a language model reads and predicts; dataset sizes are usually quoted in tokens. | 30
- **Checkpoint** — A periodic snapshot of model weights and optimizer state written during training so a job can restart after a failure. | 30
- **Post-training** — Training steps after pre-training, such as supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning, that adapt a base model. | 30
- **KV cache** — The per-token attention keys and values an LLM stores during inference so it does not recompute them for each new token. | 30
- **Time to first token (TTFT)** — The latency from submitting a prompt to receiving the first generated token, the key inference responsiveness metric. | 30
- **RAG (retrieval-augmented generation)** — Answering a prompt by first retrieving relevant documents from an index and feeding them to the model as context. | 30
- **Embedding** — A fixed-length vector of numbers that represents the meaning of a piece of content so similar items can be found by vector search. | 30
- **AI agent** — An LLM-driven program that plans and executes multi-step tasks by calling tools, APIs and data sources. | 30
- **Data gravity** — The tendency of applications, services and spending to move toward where large volumes of data already sit. | 30
- **Prefetching** — Fetching data into a faster tier before it is requested so that the consumer never waits on the slower tier. | 30
