- **Checkpoint** — A saved copy of all training state (weights, optimizer state, data-loader position) needed to resume a training run exactly where it stopped. | 31
- **Optimizer state** — The per-parameter values an optimizer such as Adam keeps (fp32 master weights and two moment estimates), which make up most of a checkpoint's size. | 31
- **Mixed-precision training** — Training that computes in low precision (bf16 or FP8) while keeping fp32 master weights and optimizer state for numerical stability. | 31
- **Adam optimizer** — The standard optimizer for large models, which stores a running mean (first moment) and running variance (second moment) of each parameter's gradient. | 31
- **Synchronous checkpointing** — Pausing training until the checkpoint is fully written to durable storage. | 31
- **Asynchronous checkpointing** — Copying training state to host memory, resuming training at once and writing the checkpoint to storage in the background. | 31
- **Local checkpointing** — Writing each node's checkpoint shard to its own SSD or RAM disk and replicating it to peer nodes before or instead of writing to shared storage. | 31
- **Checkpoint drain** — The background transfer of a checkpoint from host memory or local NVMe to durable shared storage. | 31
- **Restore storm** — The burst of simultaneous reads and metadata operations when every GPU in a job loads the latest checkpoint after a failure. | 31
- **Young's formula** — The classic approximation for the optimal checkpoint interval, the square root of two times the checkpoint cost times the mean time between failures. | 31
- **Mean time between failures (MTBF)** — The average time a system or job runs between failures; for large training jobs it shrinks roughly in proportion to GPU count. | 31
- **Effective training time** — The share of elapsed time a training job spends making useful progress, after saves, failures, restarts and rework. | 31
- **Mixture of experts (MoE)** — A model design that activates only some of its parameters per token, so compute follows active parameters while checkpoint size follows total parameters. | 31
- **Data-parallel replica** — One full copy of the model in a data-parallel training job; replicas process different data and synchronize gradients. | 31
- **Model FLOPs utilization (MFU)** — The fraction of a GPU's peak arithmetic throughput a training job actually uses on model math. | 31
