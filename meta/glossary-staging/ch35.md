- **Neocloud** — A GPU-specialist cloud provider that rents AI clusters (bare metal, Kubernetes or Slurm) to labs, enterprises and hyperscalers, e.g. CoreWeave, Nebius, Lambda, Crusoe. | 35
- **AI factory** — NVIDIA's term for a data centre whose GPUs, networks, storage and software are designed as one production line whose output is AI tokens. | 35
- **NCP (NVIDIA Cloud Partner)** — NVIDIA's programme, requirements and reference designs for GPU cloud providers, including a storage certification tier. | 35
- **NVL72** — NVIDIA's rack-scale system joining 72 Blackwell GPUs by NVLink into one liquid-cooled rack drawing roughly 120–130 kW. | 35
- **Back-end compute fabric** — The InfiniBand or Ethernet network that carries GPU-to-GPU traffic between racks in an AI cluster, kept separate from storage and front-end traffic. | 35
- **Front-end network** — The Ethernet network that carries tenant access, APIs, data ingest and management traffic into an AI cluster. | 35
- **Storage network** — The network, often converged with the front-end, that links GPU nodes to shared storage for training reads, checkpoints and KV-cache spill. | 35
- **LOTA (Local Object Transport Accelerator)** — CoreWeave's cache on each GPU node's local NVMe that accelerates reads from its AI Object Storage. | 35
- **Sell-through** — The share of a neocloud's installed storage capacity actually billed to tenants, which drives its storage gross margin. | 35
- **ClusterMAX** — SemiAnalysis's rating system for GPU clouds, scoring providers on ten criteria including storage. | 35
- **Take-or-pay contract** — A multi-year commitment in which a customer pays for reserved GPU capacity whether or not it uses it, used by neoclouds to secure financing. | 35
- **Hard multi-tenancy** — Isolation of many customers' data, performance and administration on one shared system, enforced by the platform rather than by trust. | 35
