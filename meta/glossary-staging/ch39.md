- **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** — Open protocol, introduced by Anthropic in 2024 and now governed under the Linux Foundation, that standardises how AI agents discover and call external tools and data sources. | 39
- **MCP server** — A service that exposes tools, resources and prompts to AI agents through the Model Context Protocol. | 39
- **Agentic AI** — AI systems that plan and carry out multi-step tasks by calling tools and data sources, not just answering a single prompt. | 39
- **Semantic layer** — A governed set of business definitions (metrics, dimensions, joins) that maps business terms such as "net revenue" onto underlying tables. | 39
- **Data catalog** — An inventory of an organisation's datasets recording their schema, owner, classification and lineage. | 39
- **Data lineage** — The record of where data came from, how it was transformed and which outputs or decisions it fed. | 39
- **Data security posture management (DSPM)** — Software that discovers and classifies sensitive data across an estate and flags where it is exposed or over-permissioned. | 39
- **Content-derived metadata** — Metadata produced by reading and classifying the content itself, such as "contains PII" or "EU customer record". | 39
- **On-behalf-of authorisation** — Access control in which an agent's effective rights are the intersection of the delegating user's rights and the agent's own scope. | 39
- **Confused deputy** — A security flaw in which a privileged service is tricked into using its authority for a caller who lacks it. | 39
- **Policy decision point** — The component that evaluates an access request against policy and returns allow, deny or mask. | 39
- **Open Semantic Interchange (OSI)** — A Snowflake-initiated, multi-vendor open specification for exchanging semantic-layer definitions, first published in January 2026. | 39
- **High-risk AI system** — Under the EU AI Act, an AI system used in a listed sensitive area (such as hiring or credit) that must meet data-governance, logging and oversight obligations. | 39
- **Digital Omnibus on AI** — EU Regulation 2026/1744, which amended the AI Act and postponed its high-risk obligations to December 2027 and August 2028. | 39
- **Agent rollback** — Undoing changes an AI agent made to files, databases or configurations, typically by restoring from snapshots or backups. | 39
