- **External enterprise storage systems** — Branded storage arrays sold as standalone systems to enterprises, the scope of IDC's headline quarterly storage tracker. | 40
- **External OEM ESS** — IDC's label for the external enterprise storage systems market, measured as vendor revenue by brand. | 40
- **Total enterprise storage systems** — External arrays plus server-based storage and ODM-direct hyperscaler purchases; IDC no longer publicises this total. | 40
- **ODM direct** — Storage or server hardware built by an original design manufacturer to a hyperscaler's own design and sold to it directly, bypassing branded OEMs. | 40
- **Server-based storage** — Drives inside general-purpose servers managed by software (HCI, SDS or a hyperscaler file system) rather than a dedicated array. | 40
- **TAM (total addressable market)** — The total annual spend a product category could capture if it won every deal in its defined scope. | 40
- **SAM (serviceable addressable market)** — The part of a TAM a vendor can actually sell into given its products, channels and geographies. | 40
- **Price/volume decomposition** — Splitting revenue growth into change in price per unit (e.g., per TB) and change in units shipped. | 40
- **Pull-forward demand** — Orders placed earlier than needed to beat expected price increases, which inflates current growth and borrows from future quarters. | 40
- **Magic Quadrant (MQ)** — Gartner's annual vendor assessment plotting ability to execute against completeness of vision; a perception signal, not market share. | 40
- **Enterprise Storage Platforms (Gartner)** — Gartner's merged MQ category, from 2025, covering primary block plus file and object storage. | 40
- **AI infrastructure tracker (IDC)** — IDC's quarterly measure of server and storage spending deployed for AI workloads. | 40
- **Hyperscaler self-build** — A hyperscaler designing its own storage software and buying commodity servers and drives instead of branded arrays. | 40
