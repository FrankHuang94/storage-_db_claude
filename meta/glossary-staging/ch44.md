- **EXAScaler** — DDN's commercial distribution of the Lustre parallel file system, with management tooling, usually sold on DDN appliances. | 44
- **A³I (Accelerated, Any-scale AI)** — DDN's family of EXAScaler appliances and reference designs sized for NVIDIA GPU clusters. | 44
- **AI400X series** — DDN's 2U all-NVMe EXAScaler appliances (AI400X, AI400X2, AI400X3, AI400X3M), each generation raising bandwidth per box. | 44
- **Infinia** — DDN's software-defined data platform, built on a distributed key-value store, that serves S3, SDK, KV-cache and limited POSIX access. | 44
- **Bε-tree (B-epsilon tree)** — A write-optimised B-tree whose internal nodes buffer pending updates and push them down in batches, turning small random writes into large sequential ones. | 44
- **DDN CORE** — DDN's control plane and data fabric, launched in November 2025, that spans EXAScaler and Infinia on-premises and in clouds. | 44
- **Whamcloud** — The Lustre engineering company bought by Intel in 2012 and revived as a DDN division after DDN bought Intel's Lustre business in 2018. | 44
- **Google Cloud Managed Lustre** — Google's first-party managed parallel file system service, built on DDN EXAScaler and generally available since July 2025. | 44
- **KV-cache offload** — Storing a transformer's key/value tensors outside GPU memory so that a repeated prompt prefix can be reloaded instead of recomputed. | 44
- **GPU-initiated data access** — A data path in which GPU threads issue storage operations directly, without a CPU round-trip for each request. | 44
- **Hierarchical keyspace tenancy** — Multi-tenancy implemented by nesting tenants and datasets inside a key-value namespace, with a resource share assigned to each tenant. | 44
- **Tintri** — A virtualisation-focused storage vendor whose assets DDN bought out of bankruptcy in 2018 and still sells as Tintri VMstore. | 44
- **IntelliFlash** — The all-flash array line (ex-Tegile) that DDN bought from Western Digital in 2019. | 44
- **Performance density** — Storage throughput or GPUs fed per rack unit or per kilowatt, the metric DDN emphasises in benchmarks. | 44
