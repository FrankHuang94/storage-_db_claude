- **Technical due diligence** — Verification of a target's technology claims, risks and integration cost before a deal is signed. | 50
- **Proof of concept (PoC)** — A hands-on test of a vendor's system on the buyer's own workloads, ideally on the buyer's hardware. | 50
- **Clean team** — A ring-fenced group allowed to review competitively sensitive material, such as source code, during a deal. | 50
- **Software composition analysis** — Automated scanning of a code base to inventory its open-source components and their licences. | 50
- **SBOM (software bill of materials)** — A machine-readable list of the components and licences inside a piece of software. | 50
- **Copyleft** — Licence terms, as in GPL and AGPL, that require derived works to be distributed under the same terms with source available. | 50
- **GPLv2** — The GNU General Public License version 2, a copyleft licence used by Lustre and the Linux kernel. | 50
- **Data-loss incident log** — A vendor's record of every case of lost or corrupted customer data, with root cause and blast radius. | 50
- **Field failure data** — Measured failure rates and rebuild histories from deployed systems, as opposed to modelled reliability. | 50
- **Key-person risk** — Dependence on a few people, often core code authors, whose departure would stall the product. | 50
- **Qualification matrix** — The set of server, NIC, drive, kernel and cloud combinations a software vendor must test and support. | 50
- **Integration seam** — One of the places an acquired product must join the acquirer: control plane, data path, licensing or channel. | 50
- **Benchmark normalisation** — Restating a performance claim per node, rack unit, kW or usable TB so it can be compared fairly. | 50
- **Placeholder score** — A neutral score assigned in a scorecard where public data is silent, flagged for diligence access. | 50
- **Customer concentration** — The share of a vendor's revenue that comes from its largest customers. | 50
- **Blended gross margin** — A company's overall gross margin as the revenue-weighted mix of higher-margin software and lower-margin hardware. | 50
