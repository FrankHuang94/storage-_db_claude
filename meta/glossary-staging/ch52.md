- **NOR flash** — A flash memory architecture with cells wired in parallel, giving fast random reads at low density; used mainly for code storage. | 52
- **NAND flash** — A flash memory architecture with cells wired in series strings, trading random-read speed for much higher density and lower cost per bit. | 52
- **Bits per cell** — The number of bits stored in one flash cell (SLC 1, MLC 2, TLC 3, QLC 4, PLC 5), set by how many voltage states the cell must distinguish. | 52
- **V-NAND** — Samsung's name for its 3D NAND, first mass-produced at 24 layers in 2013. | 52
- **BiCS** — Toshiba/Kioxia's 3D NAND architecture, announced in 2007 and now built jointly with Sandisk. | 52
- **Wafer spot price** — The open-market price for unpackaged NAND wafers, quoted per die-equivalent (e.g., per 512Gb unit), a fast-moving indicator of NAND pricing. | 52
- **Contract price** — The negotiated price large buyers pay NAND suppliers, usually set monthly or quarterly and slower-moving than spot prices. | 52
- **Bit growth** — The year-on-year increase in total NAND (or DRAM) bits supplied or demanded, the key supply-demand variable in memory markets. | 52
- **Wafer starts** — The number of wafers a fab begins processing per month, the physical measure of memory capacity. | 52
- **Memory cycle** — The recurring boom-bust pattern in which high memory prices fund new capacity that later overshoots demand and crashes prices. | 52
- **IMFT (IM Flash Technologies)** — The Intel–Micron NAND (later 3D XPoint) manufacturing joint venture in Lehi, Utah, formed in 2006 and unwound in 2019. | 52
- **Solidigm** — The SK hynix subsidiary formed from Intel's NAND and SSD business in December 2021. | 52
- **Kioxia** — The NAND maker formerly called Toshiba Memory, sold to a Bain-led consortium in 2018 and listed in Tokyo in December 2024. | 52
- **Effective $/GB** — Storage price divided by logical capacity after data reduction, as opposed to raw or usable capacity. | 52
- **Performance HDD** — 10K and 15K RPM enterprise hard drives optimised for IOPS rather than capacity, largely displaced by flash. | 52
- **Short-stroking** — Using only a hard drive's outer tracks to cut seek time, sacrificing most of its capacity for performance. | 52
- **HHI (Herfindahl–Hirschman Index)** — A market-concentration measure equal to the sum of squared market shares; above 2,500 is usually treated as highly concentrated. | 52
