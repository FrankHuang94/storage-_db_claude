## 1. Corporate history

### Founding and early years
- The company was incorporated in Delaware in October 2009 as OS76, Inc. and renamed Pure Storage in January 2010. — Source: SEC, "Pure Storage, Inc. Form S-1", 2015-08-27, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001474432/000119312515287576/d895532ds1.htm (retrieved 2026-11-01)
- The founders were John "Coz" Colgrove (previously at Veritas, and an entrepreneur-in-residence at Sutter Hill Ventures in 2009) and John Hayes (previously in Yahoo's Office of the Chief Technologist). The company was incubated in Sutter Hill's offices. — Source: CNBC, "Disruptors in 2014: Pure Storage", 2014-06-17, https://www.cnbc.com/2014/06/17/disruptors-in-2014-pure-storage.html (retrieved 2026-11-01); Wikipedia, "Everpure", accessed 2026, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everpure (retrieved 2026-11-01)
- Scott Dietzen became CEO in 2010 and led the company through its IPO. [VERIFY: the 2010 start date comes only from channel press] — Source: ChannelE2E, "New Pure Storage CEO: Former Cisco Systems CTO Charles Giancarlo", 2017-08, https://channele2e.com/news/new-pure-storage-ceo-former-cisco-systems-cto-charles-giancarlo (retrieved 2026-11-01)
- FlashArray launched in May 2012, together with the "All Software Included" licensing model. — Source: SEC, "Pure Storage, Inc. Form S-1", 2015-08-27, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001474432/000119312515287576/d895532ds1.htm (retrieved 2026-11-01)
- The Evergreen program (non-disruptive controller upgrades on subscription) started in early 2015. Its first tier was "Evergreen Gold", renamed Evergreen//Forever in 2022. — Source: IDC/Pure Storage, "Evergreen//Forever Subscription Ushers in New FlashBlade//S" (white paper), 2022, https://www.purestorage.com/content/dam/pdf/de-de/white-papers/wp-idc-evergreen-forever-subscription-ushers-in-new-flashblade-s-de-de.pdf (retrieved 2026-11-01)
